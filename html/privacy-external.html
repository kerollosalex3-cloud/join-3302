Privacy Policy

Join is a learning project intended for demo use. Please do not enter real or confidential information. This policy explains what personal data is processed when you use Join and the rights you have in relation to that data.

2. Hosting and access data

The website is provided using Firebase Hosting by Google. When you visit the website, technical access data may be processed, such as your IP address, the time and address requested, and technical information about your browser and request. This is necessary to operate and provide the website.

The legal basis for processing hosting and access data is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is operating and providing the website.

3. Account and app data

Firebase Authentication is used for email-and-password sign-in and anonymous guest sign-in. Firebase Authentication processes the sign-in credentials. Passwords are processed through Firebase Authentication and are not stored in the profile in the Realtime Database. A profile may contain a name and email address. Guest accounts are anonymous.

Tasks and contacts you enter in the app are also app data. The Firebase Realtime Database is configured for the europe-west1 region. According to the operator, the legal basis for account and app data is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent). You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future.

4. Tasks, contacts, and access permissions

Tasks are stored in a shared tasks collection and contacts in a shared contacts collection. Tasks contain the UID of the account that created them; contacts do not contain a creator UID. When a task is opened for editing, the task and its ID are temporarily stored in the browser's sessionStorage.

Under the currently active database rules confirmed by the operator, all signed-in accounts, including guest accounts, can read all user profiles and read and modify all tasks and contacts. An account can modify only its own user profile. Tasks and contacts are therefore not protected on a per-account basis: other signed-in accounts can view and modify them. For this reason, do not enter real or confidential information.

5. Recipients and transfers to third countries

Google and Firebase process data to the extent necessary to provide hosting, authentication, and database services. According to the operator, the Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service and the Firebase Data Processing Addendum.

Where a relevant transfer to a third country takes place and the transfer falls within the applicable scope, Google relies, according to information reviewed by the operator, on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

6. Deletion and retention

Account deletion requests are handled manually. Tasks associated with an account are checked using the stored creator UID. Since shared contacts do not contain a creator UID, they are reviewed manually as part of the request. Please identify any relevant contacts in your request. Privacy requests are received through Gmail at the email address above and are deleted after they have been handled, unless a legal retention obligation applies.

No specific project-level retention period for hosting access logs has been confirmed. According to the current screenshot, Cloud Logging is not linked to the project. Firebase specifies 30 days as the default for Hosting web request logs exported to Cloud Logging; this does not establish the current retention period for this project. No specific provider-level retention period is stated here.

7. Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, you have the right to access your personal data and request its rectification or erasure, restriction of processing, and portability. Where processing is based on legitimate interests, you may object for reasons relating to your particular situation. You may withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, particularly in the country of your habitual residence, place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement.

For privacy-related requests, please contact the controller listed above.

8. Updates

This Privacy Policy will be updated if the features, providers, or purposes of processing change.
